# Product Context

This document grounds the AI and the design system in the core goals of the product.

**Brand / Product Name**: RummyBooky
**Surface Type**: Product (App UI)
**Target Audience**: Rummy players, card game enthusiasts, and friend groups who want to track their game sessions and long-term statistics.

## Core Workflows
1. **Starting a New Game**: Quickly set up a new Rummy game, adding up to 6 players and defining the score limit.
2. **Tracking Live Games**: Log scores for each round, tracking highest hand and lowest hand during active gameplay.
3. **Leaderboard & Statistics**: View lifetime statistics including games played, won, lost, drawn, forfeited, and total lifetime score.

## Non-Goals
- Real-time online multiplayer gameplay (this is a physical game tracker, not a playable digital game).
- Complex social network features like friends lists or chat.

## Competitors & Anti-References
- **Generic Scorekeeping Apps**: Often lack specific stats (highest/lowest hand) or feel clunky. RummyBooky aims to be specialized, tactile, and highly polished specifically for Rummy.
